## These templates were made fillable by us

OREA publishes these as flat PDFs — no AcroForm fields, nothing for the fill
pipeline to write into. Rather than block on sourcing WEBForms exports, the
fields were generated by `scripts/add_form_fields.py`, which locates each dot
-leader blank from the rendered text and places a text field over it.

That means the field positions are inferred, not authoritative. They were
checked by filling each form and reading the result, but if you ever replace
one of these with a real WEBForms export, regenerate its
`forms/schemas/<form>_raw.json` and re-point the `targets` in
`intake_form_schema.json`, because the field names will differ.

## Notes

- **320 is the buy-side twin of 324** (already mapped for the lease set) —
  same layout, "Buyer/Seller" instead of "Tenant/Landlord". Likewise **371 is
  the twin of 372**. Expect much of that mapping to transfer, but verify field
  IDs rather than assuming — the lease and purchase variants are separate
  documents and OREA doesn't guarantee matching internal names.
- **101 is the big one** (6 pages) and is the purchase analogue of 400.
- 303 and 801 are short and mostly party names + dates.
